Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Eazimomo app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Gbekonzy (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects specific information when you download, sign in, and use it to provide its core functionalities. The information collected includes:

  • Email Address: Collected securely via Google Sign-In to manage your account and link your subscription or trial to your identity.
  • Device Identifiers: We collect your device's Android ID and a hashed version of your Firebase Installation ID and hardware fingerprint. This is used strictly for fraud prevention and to manage our one-time free trial system across devices.
  • Payment Information: If you upgrade to a premium plan, we process transaction references, plan selections, and payment amounts.

Data stored locally (Not transmitted):

  • Transaction Logs & Custom Shortcuts: Your generated USSD shortcuts, phone numbers entered for transactions, and USSD responses are saved entirely on your local device storage and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Contact Names: The Application may request permission to read your contacts to allow you to easily pick a phone number for a transaction. Your contacts are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device, nor does it track the specific pages you visit or the time spent on the app.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.


The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices, and marketing promotions.


Use of Accessibility Service API

The Application requires the use of the Android AccessibilityService API to provide its core functionality.

  • Why it's used: The Accessibility Service is used exclusively to automate multi-step USSD codes and read the text content of USSD dialog pop-ups (such as network transaction confirmations) to automatically log your transaction status.
  • Data accessed: The service only reads the text presented in USSD system dialogs.
  • Privacy of data: The data read by the Accessibility Service is saved securely in the Application's local database on your device. None of the data read by the Accessibility Service is ever transmitted, shared, or uploaded to our servers.
